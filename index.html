About

I trained as a joiner in Vermont and have been building furniture on my own for eleven years. Everything is made to the room it's going into — measured on site, drawn by hand, cut once.

I work mostly in white oak, walnut, and ash, sourced from mills within a day's drive. No veneer, no particleboard, no finish I wouldn't put on my own table.
